Vivian Adjeikaa Doe

Ph.D. student in Rhetoric, Theory and Culture and Graduate Teaching Assistant in First Year College Composition at North Dakota State University, Fargo, ND.

Theatre artist and drama educator (M.F.A. in Drama and Film Studies, The Central Academy of Drama, Beijing; B.A. in Theatre Arts, University of Education, Winneba). Research on community theatre and civic participation, social media and democracy, gender and media, and African digital activism, published in the South African Theatre Journal, Women’s Studies in Communication and the Howard Journal of Communications.
